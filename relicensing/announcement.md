## Moth is now GPLv3

Moth’s own code and documentation are now free and open source under the GNU
General Public License, version 3 or later (`GPL-3.0-or-later`). A recognised
open-source licence makes the terms easier to understand and keeps distributed
modified versions open under the GPL.

Nothing changes about using Moth. The official app remains free, with signed,
notarised downloads and installation through Homebrew.

You may modify and redistribute the code, including commercially, under the
GPL’s terms. When distributing binaries, provide the corresponding source as
required by the GPL. Modified forks must use a different name, icon and logo
and must not imply that they are the official Moth.

Third-party components keep their existing licences. The bundled Wumpus Mono
Pro font files remain proprietary and have their own permission; the GPL does
not cover them.

Earlier releases retain the Moth Source-Available Licence 1.0 and their original
font permission. The new terms do not revoke those earlier grants.
